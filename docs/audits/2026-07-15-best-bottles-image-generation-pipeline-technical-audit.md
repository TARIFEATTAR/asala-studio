## 1. Executive summary

Today, Madison's active Best Bottles catalog-master path is a **reference-locked, image-to-image generative edit followed by browser-side framing normalization and QA**. It is not a 3D renderer, PSD-layer compositor, segmented glass renderer, or deterministic product reconstruction system.

In plain language:

1. The operator selects a Best Bottles SKU and an approved product-reference role in the Studio UI. The two product roles are now intended to be independent: `identity-cap-on` and `pdp-cap-off-sidecar`.
2. Madison resolves the product identity and canonical measurements from catalog/manifests. Body geometry is family × size; cap-on height remains variant-specific.
3. Frontend preflight rejects unresolved identity and retired transparent/background-removed reference types. It compiles the v6.1 Best Bottles prompt and attaches one exact product reference plus, optionally, one style-only glass/metal calibration reference.
4. A Supabase Edge Function revalidates the contract. For a locked Best Bottles master it forces OpenAI `gpt-image-2`, exact `2080 × 2288`, high quality, PNG, exactly one product reference, no background reference, and at most one style reference.
5. The Edge Function calls OpenAI `/v1/images/edits`. No mask, seed, ControlNet, depth map, normal map, or denoise/strength parameter is sent. The model is asked to recreate/enhance the complete flattened product and scene.
6. The raw PNG is uploaded to the public Supabase `generated-images` bucket and a `generated_images` row is inserted.
7. The browser downloads that image and runs the family rig. The rig detects the foreground, may normalize the Bone background, rescales/translates/recanvases the full raster, checks fill height, baseline, centerline, cropping, and model-owned shadow evidence, uploads a rigged derivative, and records reconciliation evidence.
8. Human review and guarded database approval are required before Shopify publication. Shopify publishing uses a server-issued, single-use authorization and only `approved-keep` records should be eligible.

The pipeline can produce polished catalog candidates and has useful identity, framing, lineage, and approval safeguards. It cannot guarantee exact product geometry because the entire flattened product is regenerated by a non-deterministic image model. Clear glass, internal hardware, small cap differences, and detached components are therefore the hardest cases.

- Generic image routing can call Gemini or Freepik; locked Best Bottles masters do not fall back to them.

## 3. Exact end-to-end workflow: `GB-SPR-CLR-3ML-BLK`

The representative product is Grace SKU `GB-SPR-CLR-3ML-BLK`, website SKU `GBSpry3mlClBlk`, product ID `BB-GB-003-0002`: a clear 3 ml Cylinder fine-mist sprayer. Product-truth evidence independently reconciles the website identity as a 3.3 ml clear glass bottle with black spray pump and clear cap. No matched legacy cross-lane record was found in that product-truth audit, so that absence remains an evidence limitation.

### 3.1 Inputs and metadata

The current catalog row carries family, capacity, color, applicator, cap style/color, neck finish, descriptions, and crosswalk identifiers. Canonical geometry is **body 37 × 14 × 14 mm** and **assembled height 54 mm**. The catalog-lite `diameter` field exists, but this audit treats the canonical body-geometry source as authority.

The role-promotion manifest contains two separate source pointers:

- Cap-on: approved opaque 6000 × 4000 PNG, SHA-256 `30219a2e8a6034fb4b55bcbcbcd76d8ed0bd0c60f02cc5bd1071a5286759cb3a`, topology `assembled-cap-on`.
- Sidecar: opaque 360 × 480 PNG, SHA-256 `cb57723673c9389aab618be65980117137b6b77c8146e9b998e7abd325719ef5`, topology `fitment-attached-cap-right-sidecar`, sourced from exact live-PDP sidecar evidence.

The sidecar record is remotely verified and generation-authorized while its source-review field remains pending and it is below the code's general one-megapixel canonical-reference floor. That is a real contract inconsistency: one manifest authorizes it while `getBestBottlesCanonicalReferenceIssue()` would reject a generic 360 × 480 canonical source. The exact route taken by the UI determines which gate wins.

### 3.2 Selection and preprocessing

The chosen role must resolve to a fetchable PNG/JPEG/WebP URL. The active Edge byte loader:

- accepts HTTPS or supported data URLs;
- fetches the original bytes;
- limits each reference to 5 MB and all references to 12 MB;
- orders product, then background, then style references;
- does **not** resize, crop, compress, sharpen, upscale, background-remove, flatten alpha, segment, or create masks before OpenAI.

Thus the native 360 × 480 sidecar is not automatically enlarged into a 2080 × 2288 reference canvas before the provider call. The model sees those native bytes and the prompt asks it to place the product on the target canvas.

### 3.3 Prompt and model call

Frontend preflight compiles:

```text
catalog identity/material canon
+ family framing profile and canonical scale zone
+ model-owned shadow topology
+ clear-glass optical cue
+ studio direction
+ role-specific topology instruction
+ visual-target style calibration block
```

The locked request is then sent to the Edge Function. The function rejects unresolved identity, background refs, multiple product refs, masks, or retired transparent control refs. Measurement incompleteness is a warning rather than an automatic generation block.

The provider request is multipart form data:

```text
POST /v1/images/edits
model=gpt-image-2
prompt=<precompiled prompt after Madison constraint rewrites>
n=1
size=2080x2288
quality=high
background=auto
output_format=png
user=<authenticated user identifier when available>
image[]=<exact product reference bytes>
image[]=<optional style-only calibration reference bytes>
```

No seed, mask, `input_fidelity`, strength, denoise, ControlNet, depth, edge, normal, or segmentation conditioning is sent.

### 3.4 Returned image, postprocess, validation, and storage

The Edge Function trusts exact-size GPT Image 2 output and skips a second server-side decode/re-encode to avoid Edge CPU/memory failures. It uploads the raw PNG and inserts a `generated_images` row. The stored `final_prompt` is Madison's submitted prompt, not guaranteed provider-internal rewritten text.

The browser rig then downloads the public URL. With no mask, it detects product pixels heuristically, normalizes source-plate color toward `#F6EFE8`, computes framing transforms, and redraws the entire raster on a 2080 × 2288 Bone canvas. This can resample product pixels. It does not separately reconstruct glass, pump, tube, or cap. For v6.1 the model owns the shadow; the rig measures/preserves evidence rather than intentionally painting a new shadow.

Framing QA checks the full visible product envelope and, for a detached cap, uses primary-bottle bounds for centerline. The cap has no separate bounding-box requirement. The shared baseline applies to bottle and sidecar.

Failures return an error to the UI; there is no durable retry job. A user or batch script can submit again. The family batch runner defaults to two attempts, uses a 1.5-second × attempt backoff, retries geometry-QA failures, and intentionally preserves shadow-only misses for review. The Edge insert helper may retry after removing an unavailable database column, but that is schema compatibility—not a second image generation.

## 5. Prompt system

Prompt authority is split across:

1. `bestBottlesCatalogCanon.ts` — preservation, material, studio direction, negative language.
2. `bestBottlesFamilyProfiles.ts` — canvas, fill range, baseline, centerline, family scale zone, glass-volume cue.
3. `bestBottlesPromptPreflight.ts` — product inference, shadow owner/topology, QA record.
4. role-specific remediation prompt fragments — cap-on, sidecar, or approved assembled exception.
5. `bestBottlesVisualTarget.ts` — style-only glass/aluminum reference and composition-safety block.
6. optional request-level prohibited-term/constraint rewrites.

The provider has no separate negative-prompt field in this path; negatives are plain prompt prose. There is no local prompt-length cap. OpenAI may internally revise the prompt, but Madison does not persist the full provider-revised version.

## 6. Reference-image handling

- Locked lane: exactly one product ref; zero background refs; zero or one style-only ref.
- First provider image is the product authority; the optional second image is style-only. Order therefore matters in implementation and prompt semantics.
- Reference bytes are native resolution; no provider-side preprocessing is performed by Madison.
- Transparent PNG decoding may be technically possible, but transparent/background-removed product refs are contractually retired and rejected for this lane.
- The original background is sent as part of the flattened source image. Alpha is not decomposed into glass transmission.
- There is no label/body/cap/pump segmentation and no product mask in the active request.
- Duplicate/conflicting refs are prevented mainly by limiting the request to one product ref. Conflicts inside that one flattened image cannot be resolved programmatically.
- Poor native source resolution directly reduces evidence available to the model. The model is asked to invent high-frequency detail when the source lacks it.

When two sources disagree, the pipeline does not vote visually. Identity must be reconciled before selection. The canonical precedence for dimensions is manual override > live-site PDP > site-confirmed catalog > group consensus. Role-specific physical topology must come from that role's exact source; a cap-off source must not stand in for cap-on, and vice versa.

## 7. Product identity and SKU logic

Structured identity exists across catalog JSON, canonical truth CSVs, readiness manifests, SKU jobs, reconciliation rows, and prompt metadata. Active fields include SKU crosswalk, family, capacity, canonical body dimensions, neck finish, color/material, applicator, cap style/color, trim color, cap state, component topology, reference source/hash, prompt version, rig version, shadow contract, and downstream job state.

The system recognizes sibling bodies by family × size and can reuse family framing profiles. It does **not** maintain a normalized reusable component graph where a particular pump, collar, cap, dip tube, or label is a separately versioned renderable asset. Shared physical components are still mostly denormalized text/reference evidence.

There is also a serious non-Cylinder canonical-data gap: `scripts/build-bestbottles-catalog-lite.ts` still maps raw height/diameter-style catalog fields and `public/data/best-bottles-catalog-lite.json` does not carry `canon_*` fields. `bestBottlesGenerationIdentity.ts` consumes that catalog-lite surface. The Cylinder role-authority path separately replaces geometry with canonical axes and seals a geometry hash, but the global multi-family path is not yet canonical-safe—especially for flat families where raw `diameter` is forbidden.

Labels and liquid state are largely prompt constraints, not deterministic component assets. Product angle is implied by the exact reference and front-facing prompt, not a 3D camera pose.

## 8. Glass and transparency mechanics

The active request uses one flattened opaque product raster. It has no transmission/specular/liquid/label/hardware masks, depth map, normal map, semantic segmentation, layered PSD, or 3D geometry.

| Optical/physical element | Current mechanism |
|---|---|
| Outside silhouette | Flattened reference + prompt; later heuristic bounds |
| Front/rear glass surfaces | Reconstructed by GPT Image 2 from pixels + prose |
| Refraction/reflection | Generated by model; not physically simulated |
| Empty/liquid state | Prompt only |
| Dip tube/internal spring | Flattened evidence + prompt; not isolated |
| Hardware behind glass | Model interpretation; no depth ordering |
| Label | Prompt-preserved/forbidden; no exact overlay lane here |
| Contact/cast shadow | Generated by model, measured by shadow QA |
| Alpha/transmission | No active semantic glass alpha/transmission layer |

White pumps and tubes can disappear, become opaque, or appear in front of glass because flattened white/translucent pixels are ambiguous against a pale background; the model must infer depth and material; no semantic masks enforce front/back ordering; and the postprocess cannot recover a component the model omitted. Background-removal/alpha cutouts made this worse in earlier experiments, which is why those refs are retired, but the underlying generative ambiguity remains.

## 9. Generation versus compositing

| Element | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| Bottle silhouette/body/glass | Generatively edited/reconstructed from flattened reference |
| Cap/pump/sprayer/roller/collar/dip tube | Generatively edited/reconstructed, not exact layer composites |
| Liquid | Prompt says absent; not mask-enforced |
| Label/logo | Model may preserve/alter; no deterministic label overlay in this master lane |
| Background/surface | Generated, then Bone-normalized by browser rig |
| Props | Prompt forbids them |
| Shadows/reflections/highlights | Generated; shadow subsequently measured |
| Camera perspective | Inherited/inferred and prompt-directed |
| Final placement/scale | Deterministic full-raster browser recanvas after generation |

The AI is therefore being asked to recreate nearly the entire product, not merely generate a scene behind preserved product pixels.

## 10. Consistency mechanisms

Implemented: exact role reference, prompt templates/version tags, canonical 2080 × 2288 canvas, family fill ranges, global body-scale curve, shared baseline, primary centerline, role topology, optional style target, forced model/quality, deterministic recanvas, Bone normalization, reference/prompt/catalog hashes in reconciliation, shadow/framing QA, human approval, and guarded publishing.

Not implemented: seed, semantic masks, edge/depth maps, ControlNet, IP-Adapter, LoRA, fine-tune, custom product model, 3D asset, deterministic glass shader, component-layer compositor, exact label overlay, or provider request/response ledger with code commit.

Repeated runs can materially change proportions, cap shape, glass thickness, internal hardware, labels, liquid hallucination, shadow, and reflections even with the same inputs. Placement after rigging is more reproducible than product pixels.

|---|---|---|
| Identity/reference contract | exact role, one product ref, no background/mask/retired refs | Blocks before provider |
| Canonical canvas | 2080 × 2288 | Contract enforced; raw exact output trusted, rig produces target canvas |
| Fill height | family range; ±0.5 percentage point tolerance | Nonpass usually normalized; reject only if >12 points beyond range or undetectable |
| Baseline | fail >8 px, warn >4 px from target | Normalizes/reviews |
| Primary centerline | fail >2.5% from target | Normalizes/reviews |
| Crop | primary bounds must remain in canvas | Fail/reject path |
| Sidecar floor | shared baseline; default 8 px tolerance | Active; no cap bounding-box gate |
| Model shadow contact | max gap 2 px; right extension target 0.20–0.30; >0.32 fail; 0.18–0.20/0.30–0.32 warn; left extension >0.12 fail; depth >3.5% canvas fail; multiple meaningful components fail | Pass/review/fail evidence; approved exception mechanism exists |
| Reference resolution/provenance | generic canonical gate expects ≥1 MP and approved provenance | Active in validation helper; some promotion manifests conflict |
| Product silhouette/closure/component/OCR/color/glass/liquid | no robust semantic classifier | Human/prompt only |

There is no automated OCR/logo comparison, exact closure classifier, semantic component counter, liquid-level detector, glass-distortion scorer, or pixel-level product-identity similarity metric in the active master lane. `detachedSidecarLaneQa` contains explicit sidecar-right/shared-floor checks, but repository usage shows it in recovery/test-runner paths, not the normal rig/UI call chain; normal generation relies on framing plus shadow QA.

Approval RPCs require reconciliation/catalog/framing/shadow evidence and protect terminal jobs from relinking. Shadow-only failures can be handled through an explicit auditable exception/revocation record, not silent bypass. Shopify writes require a server-issued authorization and `approved-keep` state.

## 13. Data and training assessment

There is no fine-tune, LoRA, embedding-based identity model, custom diffusion model, or product training dataset used at runtime. Identity is conveyed through structured metadata, prompt text, and the flattened product reference.

The source estate contains PSD-derived native exports, legacy website imagery, GIFs, low-resolution PDP PNGs, role duplicates, different cap states, and generated/review artifacts. It has known duplicates/conflicts and unresolved role slots. The role audit proves that a fetchable/approved-looking URL is not enough; role correctness must be part of provenance.

The 4000+ Photoshop/source-image estate is not automatically transformed into semantically layered generation assets by this path. Reviewed PSD-derived PNG exports are useful references, but the active provider still receives a flattened raster.

## 14. Database and storage structure

```text
best_bottles_pipeline_groups
  -> best_bottles_pipeline_sku_jobs
       -> generated_image_id / approved_image_id
       -> best_bottles_pipeline_sku_images -> generated_images.id

generated_images
  -> best_bottles_image_reconciliations (one per image)
  -> parent_image_id (refinement chain)
  -> public generated-images object

shopify_publish_authorizations
  -> pipeline_sku_job_id + generated_image_id
  -> single-use, server-only publish grant
```

Key schema surfaces:

- `generated_images`: prompt, URL, provider, references, tags, context, session/goal, parent/refinement, consistency fields.
- `best_bottles_pipeline_sku_jobs`: SKU crosswalk, family/capacity/applicator/color, reference intake, generated/approved URLs, approval, Shopify/Convex state, last error.
- `best_bottles_image_reconciliations`: source/ref hashes, prompt/catalog hashes, raw/final URLs, canvas/bounds/baseline/fill/center/transform, framing/shadow QA, lifecycle/errors.
- `best_bottles_pipeline_sku_images`: image-to-job assignment, `unreviewed|approved-keep|needs-regen|superseded`, review and publish verification.
- `best_bottles_shadow_review_exceptions` and revocations: explicit shadow-only exceptions.
- `shopify_publish_authorizations`: short-lived single-use publish permission; clients have no table access.

Storage:

- `reference-images`: public read, reference sources and visual targets.
- `generated-images`: public read, raw and rigged masters. Authenticated upload/delete policy is not visibly constrained to an organization path in the inspected migration.
- DAM buckets: general asset management, not the locked-master source of truth.

The local migration history is not a guaranteed live schema dump: several relevant migrations are archived or deleted in this dirty worktree, and generated Supabase TypeScript types omit newer surfaces. The Edge insert helper can remove missing columns and retry, which can silently discard provenance metadata under schema drift. Live deployment status was not queried.

## 15. Reproducibility assessment

Approximate regeneration is possible because prompt, provider label, reference URLs/hashes, product truth/hash, output dimensions, prompt/rig versions, and reconciliation measurements are generally retained.

Exact regeneration is not possible because no OpenAI seed exists; provider internals can change; provider-revised prompt is not persisted; exact multipart bytes/order are not stored as a generation-attempt record; request parameters are spread across code; code commit is not attached to each output; and browser pixel transforms are not serialized as a complete executable recipe.

## 17. Current strengths

- Canonical physical truth is explicit and body geometry is separated from variant cap-on height.
- Locked masters have narrow provider/reference contracts and no generic-provider fallback.
- Role-specific source defects are now measurable rather than silently substituted.
- Prompt/family/shadow/framing versions and catalog hashes provide meaningful lineage.
- Exact canvas, baseline, centerline, fill, crop, and shadow heuristics are concrete and testable.
- Raw and rigged URLs plus reconciliation evidence preserve more history than a simple overwrite.
- Terminal relink guards, approval guards, auditable shadow exceptions, and single-use Shopify authorization reduce accidental publication.
- Human review remains in the loop for the semantic failures automation cannot detect.

## 18. Current limitations

### Prompt limitations

- Long overlapping canon/v2/v6.1/fallback labels are difficult to reason about.
- Geometry/material/topology commands can conflict with style and optical cues.
- Prompt prose cannot enforce exact component identity or depth ordering.

### Model limitations

- `gpt-image-2` is non-deterministic and does not guarantee exact geometry.
- Clear glass and tiny internal hardware are inherently ambiguous in flattened pale-background imagery.
- No exposed seed/mask/strength control is used.

### Data limitations

- Low-resolution references, mixed states, duplicate sources, and unresolved role slots remain.
- A general ≥1 MP rule conflicts with some role-promotion authorization records.
- PSDs are flattened before active generation; their layers are not used as semantic controls.

### Architecture limitations

- The complete product is regenerated rather than preserved as component layers.
- No normalized component graph or label overlay.
- No durable queue/attempt ledger or unified deployed-readiness record.
- Browser postprocess resamples the entire raster and can affect product pixels.
- Public storage URLs are the default.

### Validation limitations

- No automated exact closure/component/OCR/logo/glass/tube/liquid identity validation.
- Moderate framing defects can be normalized, but semantic defects require humans.
- Production success/failure/cost rates cannot be calculated without attempt telemetry.

## Bottom line

The current pipeline is operationally sophisticated around identity contracts, framing, reconciliation, approval, and publishing safety, but its core product rendering remains a flattened, non-deterministic generative edit. The immediate blocker to safe Cylinder-wide generation is not prompt polish: it is completing the role-specific source manifest, resolving the 158 blocked role slots, and making every runtime gate consume the same signed readiness truth. Exact clear-glass and component fidelity ultimately requires component-aware deterministic assets or 2.5D/3D representation, not more prose alone.
