## 15-415 fine-mist sprayer kits

The five catalog references each contain an exposed sprayer head, a detached protective overcap, and a dip tube. They are three visual responsibilities, not one reusable plate.

The exact Photoshop archive contains all five named sprayer-head sources. Four have a composite, background, and primary object scene; the matte-silver source also contains two additional nozzle scenes. Those internal layer fragments belong to the same exposed-head responsibility unless physical review proves an independently selectable part. Photoshop layers are therefore recomposed by responsibility before any plate is created.

- The exposed sprayer head becomes a full-canvas reusable plate anchored by the physical mount axis and seat.
- The protective overcap becomes a separate full-canvas reusable plate, inspected with its non-transparent bounds centered but placed from the same physical mount axis.
- The dip tube remains body-contextual and is rendered or welded from the mount centerline to the verified interior depth for each bottle cohort.
- The flattened catalog composite remains source evidence only and can never earn geometry lock.
