THE SHADOW — extremely subtle, restrained apothecary style:
The bottle rests on a smooth surface lit by one large soft light. Add only the
quiet shadow where the bottle meets that surface: soft, close to the base,
low in contrast, fading away within a short distance. It should barely
register — just enough that the bottle reads as grounded rather than floating.
Softest possible edges. No hard outline, no long cast shadow, no dark pool, no
heavy pooling beneath the glass. The darkest point is immediately beneath the
base edge and it lightens quickly outward.

CHANGE NOTHING ELSE — this is critical:
The bottle itself is completely untouched. Identical glass, identical colour,
identical material, identical highlights and reflections, identical position on
the canvas, identical size, width, height, outline, neck, thread rings, and
base. The background stays exactly the same seamless warm cream. Do not move,
resize, re-center, re-crop, re-frame, re-light, or re-render the bottle in any
way. Only the shadow is added beneath it.
