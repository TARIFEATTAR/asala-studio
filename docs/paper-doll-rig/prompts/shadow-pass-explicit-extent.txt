Add a soft contact shadow beneath this bottle. Change absolutely nothing else.

THE SHADOW — subtle, but clearly present:
The bottle rests on a smooth surface lit by one large soft light from above and
slightly to the right. Add the quiet shadow it casts where it meets that
surface.

Size and spread, precisely:
- The shadow extends outward roughly ONE FULL BOTTLE-WIDTH to the left and to
  the right of the base — so the total shadow is about three times as wide as
  the bottle itself.
- It reaches downward and fades to nothing over a distance of about ONE SIXTH
  of the bottle's height.
- It is darkest immediately beneath the base where the glass meets the surface,
  and lightens smoothly and continuously outward in every direction.

Character: soft-edged and low in contrast throughout. No hard outline, no
crisp ellipse, no dark pool, no long dramatic cast. Restrained and elegant —
the bottle should read as grounded and sitting on a real surface, never
floating, but the shadow must stay gentle.

CHANGE NOTHING ELSE — this is critical:
The bottle itself is completely untouched. Identical glass, identical colour,
identical material and surface texture, identical highlights and reflections,
identical position on the canvas, identical size, width, height, outline, neck,
thread rings, and base. The background stays exactly the same seamless warm
cream. Do not move, resize, re-center, re-crop, re-frame, re-light, or
re-render the bottle in any way. Only the shadow is added beneath it.
